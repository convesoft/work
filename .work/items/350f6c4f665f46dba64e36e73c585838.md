## Scope

Define idempotent squash/finalization protocol that publishes retained results as an ordinary durable digest item in a surviving checkout before temporary cleanup. Keep narrative digest caller-authored and refuse unsafe cleanup while outside references remain.

## Acceptance criteria

- A finished run with no active claims can publish a caller-authored digest linked to its origin and retained outcomes.
- Interrupted finalization retries without duplicate digest items or loss of authoritative temporary files.
- Temporary records remain when outside references or unresolved obligations still need them; cleanup eligibility is explicit.
- CLI/MCP and crash/restart tests verify digest, provenance, and safe cleanup across linked worktrees.

## Mara contracts

`REQ-RUN-FINALIZATION`, `DES-SQUASH-DIGEST`, `REQ-EPHEMERAL-FILES`, `RISK-FILE-DB-CONSISTENCY`. Resolve any unsettled operation or storage details in [open questions](../../docs/open-questions.mara.md) and update canonical Mara knowledge before implementation.

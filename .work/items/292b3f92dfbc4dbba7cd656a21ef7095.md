## Scope

Define shared handoff envelopes and recoverable save/read/cancel/cleanup operations. Store caller-authored context for one or more receivers, including continuation on the same item.

## Acceptance criteria

- Inspection and claiming expose incoming context verbatim, including multiple sources and receivers.
- Closing a source, releasing or reassigning a claim, or failed completion retains context while receiver obligations remain.
- Automatic cleanup waits until all receiver obligations resolve; cancellation counts, and reopening does not resurrect deleted notes.
- Concurrent receiver changes and interrupted handoff publication are recoverable; CLI/MCP and restart tests agree.

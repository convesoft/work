## Scope

 Add repository-wide claim, inspect, release, and reassignment operations over the shared coordination identity. Settle fencing and database-loss behavior in Mara; do not infer that updating a record stops an external executor.



 ## Acceptance criteria

 - Concurrent claim attempts for the same item across linked worktrees yield one owner and a clear conflict.
- Claims carry caller identity and can be inspected, explicitly released, or reassigned with authorized recovery context.
- Stale or lost ownership is visible and recoverable without automatic expiry or mandatory heartbeat.
- Readiness explains ownership restrictions; CLI/MCP behavior and crash/restart tests agree.

 ## Mara contracts

 `REQ-CLAIM-EXCLUSION`, `REQ-CLAIM-RECOVERY`, `DES-CLAIM-CONTEXT`, `RISK-STALE-COORDINATION`. Resolve any unsettled operation or storage details in [open questions](../../docs/open-questions.mara.md) and update canonical Mara knowledge before implementation.

## Scope

Add repository-wide claim, inspect, release, and reassignment operations over the shared coordination identity. Emit a new `claims/<claim-id>.yaml` for each acquisition, retain prior claims, and check current ownership under the shared lock using claim ID plus session identity. No separate secret token or token store. Settle exact current/retired representation and operation schemas in Mara; do not infer that retiring a claim stops an external executor.

## Acceptance criteria

- Concurrent claim attempts for the same item across linked worktrees yield one owner and a clear conflict.
- Claim acquisition consumes the foundation's coordination-availability status and refuses ownership when storage is uninitialized, damaged, unreadable or incomplete, including pending recovery. Prove this through real CLI/MCP claim requests without treating unavailable storage as an empty ownership set.
- Claims carry agent/session identity and can be inspected, explicitly released, or reassigned with explicit recovery context. New ownership never repurposes an old acquisition. Current selection does not rely on caller timestamps.
- The same session reacquiring the same item gets a new claim ID; former claim/session pairs fail owner operations after release, completion, reassignment or storage recreation.
- Stale or detectably lost/corrupt ownership is visible and explicitly recoverable without automatic expiry or mandatory heartbeat. Unreadable/malformed claims never count as unclaimed. Recovery rejects former claim/session authorizations; notifications are not required for exclusion.
- Readiness explains ownership restrictions; CLI/MCP behavior and crash/restart tests agree.

## Mara contracts

`REQ-CLAIM-EXCLUSION`, `REQ-CLAIM-RECOVERY`, `DES-CLAIM-CONTEXT`, `DES-FILE-COORDINATION`, `DES-ENTITY-LIFECYCLES`, `RISK-STALE-COORDINATION`. Resolve any unsettled operation or storage details in [open questions](../../docs/open-questions.mara.md) and update canonical Mara knowledge before implementation.

## Scope

Handle the combined failure deferred when shared file storage landed in PR #14: the identity witness is missing or malformed while prior metadata remains valid; recreation publishes a new witness, then writing its metadata stage fails and leaves empty or partial bytes. Same-ID recovery refuses the stage, while a new recreation refuses `identity_mismatch`. Surviving files remain preserved, but CLI/MCP currently have no supported recovery route for this combination.

This follow-up is outside the accepted initial beta foundation and is not being started during storage/template closeout.

## Acceptance criteria

- Settle and record the bounded identity-evidence policy in DES-STORE-FOUNDATION before changing behavior; unrelated conflicting identities must still refuse.
- Reproduce the combined lost-witness and interrupted metadata-stage write through actual fault injection.
- Provide an explicit recovery route preserving prior operation, stage and entity bytes, with correct new generation/loss reporting and CLI/MCP parity.

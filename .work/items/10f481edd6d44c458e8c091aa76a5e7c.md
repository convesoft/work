## Acceptance criteria

- Every new file envelope has exact fields, types, version rules, authority and lifetime; no duplication of host versus scope facts.
- All operations specify required/optional identity inputs, selectors, results, typed failures and bounded partial/retry behavior.
- The matrix encodes the confirmed scope-review behavior: explicit resumption after ended boundaries, historical-wisp restrictions, explicit root-wisp abandonment at rotation, idle-session retirement at ending, material hosting preserved after execution disposal, read-only physical views, explicit template ancestry and rejection of cross-run wisp reparenting, stable item-addressed context and one-namespace unqualified named claim-next. These are settled product contracts, not new choices for implementation.
- Old workspace/binding, attach/detach, rebind and receiver-pruning operations are explicitly removed; no migration/dual-layout contract exists.
- DES-BETA2-WIRE is accepted only after the matrix is complete and complete schema/project validation passes.
- No unsupported capability, implementation result, or publication authorization is claimed.

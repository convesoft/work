## Scope

Register immutable run-owned actor/namespace/ID shortcuts, lifecycle retirement/draining and terminal identity records. Resolve named or explicit ownership inputs on acquire/next/mutations/release/reassign and persist local acquisitions/endings under repository-wide exclusion.

## Acceptance criteria

- No session rebind or retired-name reuse; active->retiring->retired and retirement/acquisition serialize correctly.
- Unqualified lookup stays in effective run; explicit cross-run qualification and mixed-input rejection match frozen contracts.
- Name-only owner operations atomically resolve current acquisition; explicit stale claim IDs fail; body bytes and save-context/close/end ordering remain safe.
- CLI/MCP races across hosts yield one current owner; clocks, restart and availability never release claims; stopped-executor recovery is explicit.
- Effective-context changes refuse affected claims; real connected/fault tests and format/build/lint/test/Mara checks establish evidence.

## Mara contracts

`REQ-IMMUTABLE-SESSIONS`, `REQ-SCOPED-OWNERSHIP`, `REQ-CLAIM-EXCLUSION`, `DES-IMMUTABLE-SESSIONS`, `DES-SCOPED-CLAIMS`, `VER-IMMUTABLE-SESSIONS`. Read the accepted beta.2 contracts and freeze any still-draft wire details before behavior changes.
